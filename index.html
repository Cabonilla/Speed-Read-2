    Settings and keys

    MODE
    Words shown per flash, 1 to 4.
    ALIGN
    Anchors the word to the left, centre or right marker on the rule.
    LUT
    Panel temperature. 0C is cool paper, 40C is warm.
    CUT
    Ends a group at punctuation and holds for the set time. Commas get half.
    SPI
    Refresh speed. 40MHZ swaps words instantly; 20 and 10 leave a short blank between them.
    LINES
    Focal Read only. How many lines of the text are visible: 3, 5, 9 or 15. In full page the text fills the whole height.
    ZOOM
    Focal Read only. Text size, 75% to 200%.
    TRACK
    Focal Read only. Place moves the highlight down the text; Center keeps the highlighted line in the middle. While paused, click any word to jump to it.
    PDF
    Pick a section from the PDF's outline, or a page range of up to 200 pages. Page then shows the real PDF page.
    EPUB
    Pick a section from the book's table of contents, or a range of up to 50 parts. A part is one file inside the book, usually a chapter.
    KEYS
    Space pauses and resumes. Left and right jump 25 words. Up and down change the rate by 25. F toggles full page, Esc leaves it.
